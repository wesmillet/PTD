# Phase 6 plan: Power Clear font, Platinum menus, demo mode

Unattended batch. Maps, `scripts/bw-maps.py`, `scripts/sinnoh-maps.py` and
the tileset files are off limits (another session owns them).

## 1. Font
- Power Clear / Power Clear Bold (`public/assets/fonts/`, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
  replace Pixelify Sans, the system sans-serif and the cut sheet fonts
  everywhere. `scripts/font-probe` style measurement in a headless browser
  finds the sizes that render on the pixel grid; only those sizes are used
  (`src/ui/styles.ts` exports them, theme.css mirrors them).
- No smoothing: `-webkit-font-smoothing: none; font-smooth: never` in the
  DOM; Phaser text at resolution 1 with integer positions, nearest
  filtering (pixelArt). DS colours: `#484848` text with a `#d0d0c8` shadow
  one pixel down-right on light panels; white with a dark shadow on dark
  backgrounds. `$` renders as the Pokédollar sign (the font maps it).
- The RetroFont bitmap fonts cut from the battle GUI are dropped; the
  cutter no longer emits them. Pixelify Sans and its licence file are
  removed. Credits screen gains the font credit.

## 2. Menus like Platinum
- Pause menu = Platinum start menu: a column on the right of the canvas
  with the menu icons (Pokédex, Pokémon, Bag, trainer card, Save, Options,
  Exit, plus Leave level), a red arrow cursor, Up/Down/Enter/Esc and mouse.
  Each entry opens a small Platinum-framed page: Pokédex (seen / caught
  counts), Pokémon (party list, opens the panel), Bag (items), Trainer card
  (badges, money, Pokémon count), Save (save code panel), Options (game
  speed). Exit closes the menu; Leave level exits the level.
- Dialogue box: the bottom text box (blue frame) across the width above
  the party strip; text typed letter by letter at a fixed rate, click or
  Enter completes the page then advances; the sim pauses while it is open
  (`src/ui/dialogue.ts`). Trainer intros use it: "<name> wants to battle!",
  the intro line, then play; the trainer portrait sits at the left of the
  box.
- Pokémon panel laid out like Platinum's summary page: a header strip with
  name, Lv and gender-less species line, the portrait at the left, type
  badges, the HP box with numbers, EXP with "to next Lv", then the moves
  as rows with type badge, PP x/y and power; actions below.

## 3. No levels on the map
Remove the Lv labels from placed Pokémon and enemies; keep HP bars and
status badges. Levels stay in the panel, PC and party strip.

## 4. Drag from the party strip
Press on a party slot and drag: the Pokémon's sprite follows the pointer,
free spots highlight, releasing over a free spot places it; anywhere else
cancels. Tap-slot-then-tap-spot still works. Pointer events cover mouse and
touch. Headless test drags a slot onto a spot and checks the tower exists.

## 5. PC fills the canvas
The PC scene uses the whole 960x600 canvas: party column on the left edge,
the box (wallpaper at 3x) filling the middle, the summary side panel on the
right, no margins.

## 6 and 7. Demo mode and the showcase PC
`config.demoMode: true`: every level unlocked (`isLevelUnlocked`), and a
new game seeds the boxes from `src/data/showcase.json` (generated by
`scripts/build-showcase.mjs` from the given list: 44 Pokémon, levels 20
to 60, 12 shiny in Box 1, movesets from the Platinum learnsets). Sprites,
portraits and icons fetched for the 38 new species. GAME_DESIGN.md notes
that demo mode goes off for release.
