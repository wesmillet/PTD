Gen 5 (Black/White) style tilesets
Both compiled in "Tiles - Ready to use Tilesets" by Aki,
https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/15/
Per that page, credit the original artists, not the compiler.

kyle-dove-outside.png
  Artist: Kyle-Dove (signed on the sheet).
  Format: RPG Maker XP tileset, 256 px wide (8 tiles x 32 px), 6720 px tall.
  Contents: terrain, grass, trees, cliffs, water edges. The terrain edges
  (dirt path, sand, pond, pool, tall grass, snow, beach) are drawn into the
  sheet as 3x3 edge blocks, some with a 2x2 inner-corner block; the game
  rebuilds RPG Maker XP autotiles from them (scripts/build-tilesets.mjs).

gen5-exterior.png
  Pack entry: cmm6jjn (the name of this sheet in the source pack).
  Artist: UltimoSpriter (signed on the sheet, top and bottom).
  Format: RPG Maker XP tileset, 256 px wide (8 tiles x 32 px), 41504 px tall.
  Contents: buildings, roofs, fences, signs, town and route decoration.
  Only the flowers, bushes, weeds, grass variants, signs, lamp post, mailbox
  and grey rocks are shipped (see src/data/tiles.json).
